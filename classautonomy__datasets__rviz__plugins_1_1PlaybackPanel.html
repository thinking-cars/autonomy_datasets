signal

Reports the outcome of a sample request from the thread that received the response.

Connected to applyRequestResult() as a queued connection, so that the widgets are only ever touched by the GUI thread, no matter which thread the executor of RViz answers the request on. Qt drops the queued call if the panel is closed before it is delivered.
